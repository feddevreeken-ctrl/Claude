Risk Assessment

Risks & Mitigations

Transparent risk management is a selling point, not just a planning exercise. Clients trust us more when we're honest about our constraints.

Student Quality Inconsistency High

Variability in student skill levels could impact delivery quality.

Tiered hiring system, senior review gate on all client deliverables, peer review process.

Reputational Risk (Failed Project) High

One badly delivered project could derail the brand early on.

Only accept projects clearly within proven capability. Decline bad-fit clients proactively.

Student Churn Mid-Project Medium

Students leaving for placements, exams, or new opportunities.

2-student minimum per project, rigorous documentation, knowledge transfer sessions.

Client Data Security Medium

Handling sensitive business data with student team members.

PI insurance, data processing agreements, no raw data stored on personal devices, NDAs.

Scope Creep Medium

Clients expanding requirements without additional payment.

Strict change request process from Day 1. Fixed-scope contracts with clear boundaries.

Regulatory (AI Act / GDPR) Medium

Evolving AI regulation could expose us or clients to liability.

Legal advisor review of engagements. Avoid high-risk AI use cases in Year 1.

API Cost Overruns Low

LLM API costs exceeding budget on large projects.

Cost cap clauses in contracts, pass-through billing for production usage.

Sales Pipeline Dry Spell Low

Slow early pipeline if outbound doesn't convert quickly.

University alumni network as warm leads. First 3 clients offered discounted rates for case studies.
