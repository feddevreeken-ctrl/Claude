# NexGen AI Consulting — Business Plan
### Student-Led AI Consultancy for SME & Mid-Market Companies
**Version 1.0 | March 2026**

---

## Executive Summary

**NexGen AI Consulting** is a student-run consultancy that helps small-to-medium enterprises (SMEs) and mid-market companies adopt, implement, and scale AI tooling. Operating out of a university hub model, it leverages a rotating cohort of graduate and undergraduate students supervised by a small core of experienced professionals, keeping costs dramatically lower than traditional consultancies while delivering genuine value.

- **Target Revenue (Year 1):** £180,000 – £240,000
- **Net Margin (Year 2+):** 28–35%
- **Pricing Model:** Project-based retainers + a one-off implementation fee
- **Primary Market:** UK-based SMEs (10–250 employees) with £2M–£50M turnover
- **Core Services:** AI readiness audits, workflow automation, LLM integration, AI tool selection & onboarding, staff training

---

## 1. The Problem We Solve

Most SMEs know AI matters but have no idea where to start. They face:

| Pain Point | Reality |
|---|---|
| Too expensive to hire a full-time AI engineer | Mid-level AI engineer: £60,000–£90,000/yr salary |
| Big consultancies are out of budget | McKinsey/Accenture AI projects: £100,000–£500,000+ |
| No internal expertise to evaluate vendors | Sales pitches go unchallenged; wrong tools get purchased |
| Failed pilots that never scale | 70–85% of AI projects fail to reach production (Gartner, 2025) |
| Fear of disruption without a guide | Decision paralysis, no clear roadmap |

**NexGen fills the gap**: expert-quality consulting at SME-friendly prices, delivered by motivated students supervised by professionals.

## 2. Business Model

### 2.1 Revenue Streams

#### A) AI Readiness Audit (Entry Product)
A 2-week deep-dive into a company's current workflows, data maturity, team skills, and tech stack.

- **Deliverable:** 30-page audit report + prioritised recommendations roadmap
- **Price:** £2,500 – £4,500 (depending on company size)
- **Team required:** 2 students + 0.25 senior supervisor
- **Cost to deliver:** ~£800–£1,100
- **Gross margin:** ~70–75%

#### B) AI Implementation Project (Core Product)
End-to-end delivery of a single AI use case (e.g., automating invoice processing, building a customer support chatbot, setting up an AI-assisted CRM pipeline).

- **Price:** £8,000 – £25,000 per project
- **Duration:** 6–16 weeks
- **Team:** 3–5 students + 0.5 senior supervisor
- **Cost to deliver:** £3,500 – £8,000
- **Gross margin:** ~55–65%

#### C) Ongoing Retainer / AI Operations Support
Monthly advisory: monitoring tools, fine-tuning prompts, model updates, staff Q&A.

- **Price:** £800 – £2,500/month
- **Hours per month:** 10–20 hours (mostly student-led)
- **Cost to deliver:** £300 – £700/month
- **Gross margin:** ~60–72%

#### D) AI Literacy Training Workshops
Half-day or full-day workshops for non-technical staff (sales, ops, HR, finance) on using AI tools effectively.

- **Price:** £1,200 – £2,500 per session (up to 20 people)
- **Cost to deliver:** £400 – £600
- **Gross margin:** ~65–75%

## 3. Target Audience

### Primary Segments

**Segment 1 — Professional Services (highest readiness)**
- Accounting firms, law firms, marketing agencies, HR consultancies
- Pain: manual document processing, client communication overhead
- AI quick wins: document summarisation, contract review, email drafting
- Budget comfort: £5,000–£20,000 project spend

**Segment 2 — E-commerce & Retail**
- Online retailers with 5–100 staff
- Pain: customer service volume, inventory forecasting, product descriptions
- AI quick wins: chatbots, demand forecasting, AI-generated product copy
- Budget comfort: £4,000–£15,000

**Segment 3 — Manufacturing & Logistics (SME tier)**
- Small factories, logistics brokers, distribution companies
- Pain: quality control bottlenecks, scheduling, supplier communication
- AI quick wins: anomaly detection, route optimisation, document parsing
- Budget comfort: £8,000–£30,000

**Segment 4 — Healthcare & Care Sector (longer sales cycle but sticky)**
- Private GP practices, dental groups, care homes
- Pain: admin overload, CQC compliance documentation
- AI quick wins: transcription, appointment management, report generation
- Budget comfort: £5,000–£18,000

### Secondary Segments
- Non-profits needing low-cost AI (discounted rates, brand/case-study value)
- Local councils (longer procurement but large contracts)
- Startups needing fractional AI expertise

### Who We Do NOT Target (Year 1)
- Enterprise companies with 1,000+ employees (need too much governance overhead)
- Highly regulated finance/banking (compliance complexity beyond student bandwidth)
- Companies with no existing digital tools (too much foundational work before AI)

## 4. Services in Depth

### 4.1 What a Client Engagement Looks Like

**Week 1–2: Discovery & Audit**
- Stakeholder interviews (CEO, ops lead, frontline staff)
- Current tech stack mapping
- Data quality assessment
- Identify top 3–5 AI opportunity areas
- Risk and compliance flags

**Week 3–4: Strategy & Scoping**
- Present findings + recommended use cases
- Build business case (ROI estimate, payback period)
- Define success metrics
- Agree scope of implementation

**Week 5–12+: Build & Deploy**
- Vendor/tool selection (e.g., OpenAI API, Claude API, Microsoft Copilot, Make.com, n8n, Zapier, Dify, LangChain)
- Integration into existing software (CRM, ERP, email, Slack)
- Testing and iteration
- Handover documentation

**Week 13+: Training & Handover**
- Staff training sessions
- Admin/power-user training
- Transition to retainer (optional)

## 9. Risk Analysis

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Student quality inconsistency | Medium | High | Tiered system, senior review gate on all deliverables |
| Client data security concerns | Medium | High | PI insurance, data processing agreements, no raw data storage |
| Students leaving mid-project | High | Medium | 2-student minimum per project, knowledge documentation |
| AI tool cost overruns (API fees) | Low | Medium | Cost cap clauses in contracts, pass-through billing |
| Scope creep | High | Medium | Strict change request process from day 1 |
| Reputational risk from failed project | Low | High | Only take projects within proven capability; turn down bad fits |
| Regulatory (AI Act, GDPR) | Medium | High | Legal advisor review; avoid high-risk AI use cases in Year 1 |

## 10. Legal & Compliance

- **Company structure:** Ltd company (not LLP — simpler, lower liability)
- **Professional indemnity insurance:** minimum £1M cover (£800–£1,500/yr)
- **Data processing agreements:** required with every client under UK GDPR
- **Student contracts:** self-employed contractor agreements (IR35 awareness needed)
- **IP assignment:** all work product assigned to the company (not the student) via contract
- **EU AI Act awareness:** avoid deploying "high-risk" AI systems (biometrics, credit scoring, hiring automation) until compliance framework is in place
- **VAT registration:** register voluntarily at launch if expecting >£85,000 turnover in Year 1
